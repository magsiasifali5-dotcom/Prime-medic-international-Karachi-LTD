Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of pharmaceutical products does Prime Medic International supply?

Ans: We supply a comprehensive range of high-quality pharmaceutical products, including prescription medications, over-the-counter (OTC) drugs, specialized clinical supplies, and healthcare essentials sourced from certified manufacturers.

Q2: How do you guarantee the quality and authenticity of your medicines?

Ans: Guided by our core belief that "Quality is the basic right of everyone," we source all products directly from licensed, accredited pharmaceutical companies. Every batch undergoes strict quality control procedures and compliant temperature-controlled storage before distribution.

Q3: Are your pharmaceutical products compliant with DRAP guidelines?

Ans: Yes, absolutely. All products distributed by Prime Medic International Karachi Ltd. strictly adhere to the guidelines and regulations set by the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) and international safety standards.

Q4: Which areas or cities do you distribute to?

Ans: While our primary distribution hub is based in Karachi, we have a robust logistics network capable of fulfilling wholesale pharmaceutical orders across Sindh and nationwide throughout Pakistan.

Q5: How can a pharmacy, hospital, or clinic place a bulk order?

Ans: You can easily place an order by reaching out through our website’s Contact Form, calling our official sales helpline, or emailing our procurement department directly. Our team will assist you with product availability and quotation details.

Q6: What is your standard delivery timeline for bulk orders?

Ans: Deliveries within Karachi are typically completed within 24 to 48 hours. For nationwide orders, delivery times vary depending on the destination, but we prioritize fast-track shipping to maintain supply chain continuity.

Q7: Do you provide temperature-controlled shipping for sensitive medicines?

Ans: Yes. We use specialized cold-chain logistics and temperature-sensitive packaging to ensure that vaccines, biologics, and temperature-critical pharmaceuticals maintain their efficacy from our warehouse to your doorstep.

Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Welcome to Prime Medic International Karachi Ltd.
At Prime Medic International Karachi Ltd., accessible from our official website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and clients. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by us and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal and business information when you place a bulk order, request a quote, or contact us through our website. This includes:

  • Personal & Business Information: Name, company/pharmacy name, job title, email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Order & Transaction Details: Purchased product names, batch quantities, delivery address, billing invoices, payment methods, and transaction reference numbers.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and site navigation data collected via log files and cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process, fulfill, and deliver pharmaceutical orders safely and on time.
  • Manage customer support and respond to product inquiries or quotations.
  • Maintain accurate business records, tax invoicing, and legal compliance with regulatory authorities like DRAP (Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan).
  • Improve our website user experience and service efficiency.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement strict physical, technical, and administrative security measures to protect your personal and business data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Sensitive order and financial transaction data are handled through secure channels.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Prime Medic International Karachi Ltd. does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary data (such as delivery address and receiver contact details) with trusted logistics and courier partners to ensure the accurate and timely distribution of your orders.

5. Cookies Policy

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember user preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options if you prefer.

6. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to any inaccurate information, or ask for the deletion of your account records where legally permissible.
